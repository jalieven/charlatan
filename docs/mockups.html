Setup, ready to start

Drag handles reorder seating; the Charlatan stepper shows AUTO·1 until the host overrides (clamped 1…⌊n/3⌋, §2.1). Rows here are compressed — built targets are ≥ 56 px. On launch with an unfinished round, a "Hervatten of nieuw spel?" prompt precedes this screen (§4).

S3 · Reveal

One thumb, everything snaps back

The grammar of §3.3: covered resting state → swipe-up-hold for the word → long-press for the role card → optional slide-to-Whisper → slide to pass. Nothing secret survives a lifted finger. The civilian role card (not shown) is geometrically identical to the Charlatan's.

4 · Role card — held, Charlatan

Open only while the long-press is held. The Whisper commits by sliding the touching thumb onto the dashed target and releasing there — release anywhere else closes the card (§3.3). The civilian card shows the same two targets; both just close. The target is absent once the round's Whisper is burned — the accepted tell (§3.6).

Three acts, narrated by the phone-holder

One act per screen, advanced by tap, three-dot strip, back-swipe allowed — everything here is public. The words and the replay sit behind buttons on the verdict (§4, step 8). Scenario: round 4, Tom caught, steal missed, burgers win.

Act 3 · The damage

Per-player deltas with their reasons inline (§3.9) — tabular numerals, heaviest weight on the number. Continue lands on the scoreboard (S9).

Type note: these frames render in the system font; the built app ships Space Grotesk (or Inter Tight) per §6.5 — expect the words and verdicts to gain more character at the same weights.
